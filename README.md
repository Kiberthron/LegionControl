# ⚡ Legion Control for Decky Loader

> A lightweight, feature-rich Decky Loader plugin designed for **Lenovo Legion Go 2** handhelds to manage hardware controls, vibration profiles, touchpad haptics, and RGB lighting directly from the SteamOS Quick Access Menu (QAM).

---

## ✨ Features

* **🔋 Battery Management:** Quick toggle for battery conservation mode (80% limit) to preserve battery health when playing plugged in.
* **💡 RGB Lighting Control:** Turn joystick ring lighting on or off and switch between custom visual effects (*Static, Breathe, Chroma, Rainbow*).
* **📳 Advanced Vibration (Rumble) Tuning:** 
  * Adjust global rumble intensity levels (*Off, Low, Medium, High*).
  * Configure hardware rumble profile modes (*FPS, Racing, Standard, SPG, RPG*).
  * Instant built-in vibration testing pulse.
* **🖐️ Touchpad Haptics:** Enable/disable touchpad feedback and independently adjust intensity levels.
* **🌐 Multi-language Support:** Automatic localization for English, Ukrainian, and Russian based on your Steam client settings.

---

## 🛠️ Requirements

* Lenovo Legion Go 2 handheld device running **SteamOS** .
* **Decky Loader** installed.
---

⚠️ **Compatibility Note:** Fully compatible with **Lenovo Legion Go 2**, with **experimental** support for **Legion Go** and **Legion Go S**.
